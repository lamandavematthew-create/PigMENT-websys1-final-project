Practical guidance

Health Guide

African Swine Fever

ASF is a serious viral disease of pigs. Fever, weakness, loss of appetite, vomiting, diarrhea, skin discoloration, or sudden deaths can be warning signs, but they do not confirm ASF. Contact a veterinary authority for assessment.

General pig health

Monitor behavior, appetite, breathing, movement, and body condition. Seek veterinary guidance when changes persist, several pigs become ill, or deaths occur.

Biosecurity basics

Clean and disinfect equipment, limit visitors, isolate new or sick pigs, avoid sharing tools between pens, and keep records of pig movement.

Educational guidance only. Sources: FAO, WOAH, and Philippine veterinary authorities. Review with a local veterinary professional.

i

Public records only: PigMENT does not automatically detect or diagnose disease. Contact your municipal or provincial veterinary office for official assessment.

Know the signs

ASF awareness essentials

Possible symptoms

  • High fever, weakness, or loss of appetite
  • Red or bluish skin, especially on ears or legs
  • Vomiting, diarrhea, coughing, or sudden death

Prevention

  • Limit visitors and avoid bringing pork products into the farm
  • Do not move pigs without following local veterinary guidance
  • Report unusual illness promptly to authorities

Biosecurity habits

  • Use dedicated footwear, clothing, and clean tools
  • Clean and disinfect pens, vehicles, and equipment
  • Keep new or returning pigs separated and monitored
